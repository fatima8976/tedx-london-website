About Us
Where curiosity meets community

TEDxNortheasternU London is an independently organised TEDx event run by students at Northeastern University London. We bring together the diverse voices of our global campus to share innovative ideas that challenge, inspire, and connect.

Inspired by TED's mission to discover and spread ideas that change the world, we create a space where bold thinking is celebrated and every perspective has a place.

What is TEDx?
A grassroots initiative
“Ideas worth spreading — in your community.”

TEDx is a grassroots initiative created in the spirit of TED's overall mission to discover and spread ideas that matter. TEDx brings the spirit of TED to local communities around the globe through independently organised events.

These events are organised by passionate individuals who seek to uncover new ideas and share the latest research, sparking conversations in their communities. TEDx events include live speakers and TED Talk videos to spark deep discussion and connection in a small group.
