Executive Committee
Secretary
← Back to All Positions

The Secretary keeps TEDxNortheasternU London organised and running efficiently. You will manage internal communications, maintain records, schedule meetings, and ensure the team stays aligned and informed across all stages of the event cycle.

Location
London, UK
Time Commitment
3–5 hrs/week
Responsibilities
  • Record and distribute minutes from all team meetings
  • Manage the team calendar and coordinate scheduling
  • Maintain organised records of key documents and decisions
  • Draft and circulate internal communications and updates
  • Support the Executive Committee with administrative tasks
  • Liaise with external contacts where required
Requirements
  • Currently enrolled at Northeastern University London
  • Highly organised with strong attention to detail
  • Excellent written communication skills
  • Proficiency with Google Workspace or Microsoft 365
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to manage confidential information
