The Theme
Born at the edge of discomfort

“Ideas. Change. Everything.” explored how transformative ideas emerge when we step beyond what's familiar. Our speakers challenged assumptions across identity, confidence, inclusion, beauty, and belonging.

Through an evening of diverse talks spanning storytelling, leadership, and entrepreneurship, our inaugural event brought the spirit of TED to the Northeastern London community for the very first time.
